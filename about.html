01 / HOW IT STARTED

One skill led
to another.

I began as someone curious about how websites were made. Instead of waiting for the perfect opportunity, I started teaching myself web design, learning how HTML gives a page structure and how CSS brings that structure to life.

Each small project gave me something new to figure out. As I became more comfortable designing pages, I wanted to understand more than how a website looked. I wanted to learn how websites become interactive, how applications work, and how code can solve practical problems.

That curiosity encouraged me to take my learning further through formal education. In 2025, I reached a significant milestone by earning a diploma at the National Institute of Technology (NIT), Oshakati Campus, Namibia. Moving from self-directed learning into formal study reinforced the value of consistency, patience, and staying willing to learn.
